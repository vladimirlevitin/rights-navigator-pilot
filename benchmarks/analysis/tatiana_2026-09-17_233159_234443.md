## Batch 233159

### 1. Private use of common-building water

**Real problem:** a condominium/common-property dispute about neighbours using shared water for private purposes.

**Decision:** no Navigator knowledge card. This is outside the current social/labour/benefits pilot and would require municipal/condominium-law coverage that the verified corpus does not yet have.

### 2. Pension contributions during pregnancy safeguarding (`שמירת הריון`)

**Real problem:** the employer stopped pension contributions when the employee entered a BTL-recognised pregnancy-safeguarding period.

**Operational delta:** distinguish ordinary absence/sick leave from a recognised `שמירת הריון` period; verify the employee had the required pre-absence employment/contribution history; obtain the BTL confirmation; arrange payment of the employee share; then verify that both shares actually reached the pension account.

**New playbook:** `employment-pension-pregnancy-guarding`.

**Important practical point:** the user should not stop at “the employer must pay”. The action sequence includes giving the employer the BTL confirmation, arranging the employee contribution and checking the pension-fund statement afterwards.

### 3. Temporary long-term-care award for three months

**Real problem:** the user wants to know what happens when a temporary `סיעוד` decision approaches its end.

**Operational delta:** treat the end date as a process deadline. For a temporary award, collect fresh medical/function documents before expiry, use the BTL reassessment route at the recommended time, and check the new level and duration after the decision.

**Refined playbook:** `long-term-care-worsening-reassessment`.

**Not encoded:** a blanket rule that “after X months it becomes permanent”. Permanence depends on the decision and medical/functional prognosis.

### 4. Post-stroke non-pensioner needs daily personal assistance

**Real problem:** the family asks about discounted continence products, but the broader unmet right is assistance with daily activities.

**Operational delta:** route first to `שר״מ` when the person is below the relevant retirement boundary and needs substantial help with dressing, washing, eating, moving inside the home, personal hygiene/continence or supervision. The decisive facts are age and actual ADL dependence, not the stroke diagnosis alone.

**New playbook:** `special-services-daily-assistance`.

### 5. Disability recipient stops working and wants the full benefit restored

**Real problem:** the medical/incapacity decision already exists; only employment income changed.

**Operational delta:** obtain an employer letter with the exact cessation date, keep the last payslip, notify BTL through the personal account/contact channel, explicitly request recalculation due to lower earnings, and verify the effective month. While the incapacity degree remains valid, a fall in earnings does not by itself require a new medical committee.

**Refined playbook:** `disability-stop-work`.

### 6. Temporary inability to work after surgery while receiving partial disability benefit

**Real problem:** three different systems can be confused: BTL general disability, income support, and occupational-disability coverage from a pension/insurance fund.

**Decision:** no unsupported “extra BTL sick-pay” rule was created. The existing disability-stop-work route now covers the income change. The pension-fund loss-of-capacity route remains fund/policy-specific and should not be stated as automatic entitlement without the relevant fund rules.

### 7. Woman aged 62, still working, received a transition-grant letter

**Real problem:** a BTL letter signals possible eligibility, but work during the decisive four-month age window can defeat the grant.

**Operational delta:** before filing, check exact birth date, whether there was any work income between age 62 and 62 years 4 months, then separately check pensions/benefits, non-work income, insurance period and incompatible benefits.

**New playbook:** `old-age-transition-grant-women-62`.

### 8. Child conceived with donor sperm; single-parent status

**Real problem:** the user is unsure whether the method of conception changes recognition as an independent parent.

**Operational delta:** determine marital/cohabitation status and whether the child is in the parent’s household/support; then check each concrete benefit separately. Do not infer eligibility for every single-parent benefit from status alone.

**New playbook:** `independent-parent-unmarried-status`.

### 9. Five unpaid unemployment days inside a 300-day entitlement

**Real problem:** whether the periodic five unpaid days reduce the 300-day bank or are paid later.

**Decision:** already covered correctly by `payment-timing`: they are not paid later and do not reduce the total entitlement-day bank. Kept as a regression case; no duplicate card.

### 10. Old-age recipient works and sees income tax but no BTL/health deductions from salary

**Real problem:** payroll deductions are being conflated.

**Operational delta:** separate BTL/health-insurance deductions from income tax. Old-age-recipient status changes the former; it does not itself exempt employment income from income tax.

**Refined playbook:** `insurance-old-age-recipient-employee`.

## Batch 234443

### 1. Returning resident woman near retirement age and short insurance history

**Real problem:** Tatiana’s answer correctly points to qualifying-period alternatives but overstates the fallback as “wait until 70”.

**Operational delta:** obtain the BTL insurance-period history, test all qualifying-period alternatives, and if the period is not yet met, calculate how many insured months remain and recheck when a route becomes satisfied. Do not automatically postpone the next check until age 70.

**Refined playbook:** `old-age-qualifying-period-woman`.

### 2. Income support while studying in ulpan and a vocational course

**Real problem:** BTL requests evidence that the study arrangement actually supported the exemption from Employment Service attendance.

**Operational delta:** first identify the legal basis of the exemption, then collect programme dates/days/hours and participation confirmation for each relevant programme; explain which course caused absences; preserve proof of submission; if BTL recalculates retroactively, demand a written decision for the disputed months.

**New playbook:** `income-support-study-ulpan-proof`.

**Intentional restraint:** the Navigator does not invent a requirement for a special “ulpan permission” unless the applicable programme actually requires one.

### 3. Arnona after buying an apartment with a mortgage

**Real problem:** whether the mortgage changes municipal-tax liability.

**Decision:** no new card. The case adds little reusable operational value to the current pilot beyond ordinary municipal-tax rules, and no new social-benefit process was discovered.

### 4. New-immigrant rent assistance stopped after a long stay abroad

**Real problem:** Tatiana gives a specific 60-day absence rule and a route for resuming payment.

**Decision:** the exact 60-day rule was **not encoded** because it was not verified sufficiently in the official material reviewed in this pass. This case remains discovery material for a later targeted housing/aliyah source check.

### 5. Elderly couple’s rental contract expired while receiving housing aid

**Real problem:** payments depend operationally on having a current valid lease in the system.

**Operational delta:** check the entitlement certificate, renew the lease, submit it through the housing-aid channel, confirm bank/family data and verify the next payment.

**Refined playbook:** `housing-assistance-update-details`.

**Not encoded:** the broad claim that assistance automatically falls to a particular “minimum” after a specified period without a renewed contract.

### 6. Severance payment after dismissal while receiving unemployment benefit

**Real problem:** “severance payslip” can contain several legally different components.

**Operational delta:** separate actual severance (`פיצויים`) from wage arrears, unused leave, notice-period compensation and other final-settlement items; if unemployment is reduced or delayed, request a month-by-month BTL calculation showing which component was counted.

**New playbook:** `unemployment-severance-final-settlement`.

### 7. Age 65, wants to resign without losing severance

**Real problem:** retirement-age resignation depends materially on whether the employment relationship began before or after reaching retirement age.

**Operational delta:** compare start-of-employment date with retirement-age date, use explicit retirement wording when that is the legal basis, verify pension-fund severance deposits and Form 161, and if employment began after retirement age check a different justified-resignation ground rather than assuming age alone is sufficient.

**Refined playbook:** `severance-retirement-resignation`.

### 8. Old-age income supplement and apartment in Russia

**Real problem:** foreign real estate must be classified as an asset/income source rather than ignored because it is outside Israel.

**Operational delta:** disclose the property, document ownership/value, state whether it is rented, document actual rental income if any, and obtain the written BTL imputed-income calculation.

**New playbook:** `old-age-income-supplement-real-estate`.

**Not encoded:** Tatiana’s broader statement that Israeli rent can automatically be deducted from foreign rental income in this scenario was not established strongly enough by the official material reviewed.

### 9. Water discount missing for an income-supplement recipient

**Real problem:** entitlement exists but implementation data/address may not match.

**Decision:** existing `old-age-water-benefit` already contains the correct operational route: check address, water-corporation registration/list, then escalate the missing-list/data issue. Kept as regression, no duplicate card.

### 10. Sukkah in public/common space

**Decision:** outside the current pilot; municipal/property-specific and no new social/labour/benefit playbook was created.
